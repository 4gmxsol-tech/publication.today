Champions League Matchday 4 Results: Barcelona 3–0 Aston Villa and Real Madrid 3–2 AEK Athens

Matchday 4 delivered two major results: Barcelona beat Aston Villa 3–0 at home on November 3, while Real Madrid came from behind twice to defeat AEK Athens 3–2 on November 4.

Publication Today EditorialUpdated November 9, 2026
F

The fourth round of the 2026/27 UEFA Champions League league phase is complete. Barcelona produced a controlled home win, while Real Madrid needed a late strike to finish a five-goal contest in Athens.

Barcelona 3–0 Aston Villa: Yamal starts it, Raphinha finishes it

Lamine Yamal opened the scoring in the seventh minute after capitalising on a misplaced pass. Aston Villa created chances to respond, but Barcelona protected their lead and added two second-half goals through Raphinha, who scored in the 52nd and 64th minutes.

The official Barcelona report says the win took the club to nine points from four matches. It also notes that the team was temporarily second while other Matchday 4 fixtures were still to be played.

AEK Athens 2–3 Real Madrid: Valverde settles a late comeback

AEK took the lead through Aboubakary Koïta in the 12th minute. Jude Bellingham equalised for Madrid in the 32nd, but Stavros Pilios restored the hosts’ advantage in the 75th. Kylian Mbappé made it 2–2 three minutes later, before Federico Valverde scored the winner in the 92nd minute.

The result preserved Real Madrid’s perfect start to the league phase and lifted them to 12 points after four matches, according to Reuters’ Matchday 4 roundup.

What the results tell us

Barcelona’s win was built on an early breakthrough and Raphinha’s finishing after the interval. Real Madrid’s match was more volatile: they had to recover from two deficits and rely on a late long-range finish. Both results underline how quickly the league-phase table can move across a busy matchday.

Match facts

  • Barcelona 3–0 Aston Villa: Lamine Yamal (7'); Raphinha (52', 64').
  • AEK Athens 2–3 Real Madrid: Aboubakary Koïta (12'), Stavros Pilios (75'); Jude Bellingham (32'), Kylian Mbappé (78'), Federico Valverde (92').
  • Competition: UEFA Champions League, 2026/27 league phase, Matchday 4.
  • Dates: November 3 and 4, 2026.
