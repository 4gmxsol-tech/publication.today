Champions League Matchday 4 Preview: Barcelona vs Aston Villa and Real Madrid at AEK Athens

The UEFA Champions League returns on November 3–4. Barcelona host Aston Villa, while Real Madrid travel to face AEK Athens. Here are the key fixtures and kick-off times.

Publication Today EditorialUpdated October 25, 2026
F

The UEFA Champions League league phase returns in early November, with major clubs looking to build momentum as the table takes shape. Here are the fixtures worth marking on your calendar.

Tuesday, November 3: Barcelona vs Aston Villa

Barcelona welcome Aston Villa on Tuesday night. Barcelona will want to use home advantage and control possession, while Villa will look for opportunities to attack space quickly. Both sides’ final line-ups and player availability should be checked on matchday.

Wednesday, November 4: AEK Athens vs Real Madrid

Real Madrid travel to Greece to face AEK Athens. UEFA lists kick-off at 18:45 CET (19:45 Sudan time). Away-game management, set pieces and transition defence are key themes to watch, but the final team news should come from the clubs’ official channels.

More fixtures to watch

  • Tuesday, November 3: Atlético de Madrid vs Bayern München.
  • Wednesday, November 4: Slavia Praha vs Arsenal — 18:45 CET (19:45 Sudan time).
  • Wednesday, November 4: Fenerbahçe vs Liverpool — 18:45 CET (19:45 Sudan time).

These are selected fixtures, not the full matchday programme. Other league-phase games are scheduled across both evenings.

How to check kick-off times and team news

Fixture times can change and broadcast rights vary by country. Before making plans, confirm the latest details on UEFA’s official 2026/27 Champions League fixtures page and the clubs’ official channels. This preview was prepared on October 25, 2026; it is not a live-score or post-match report.

Source: UEFA — 2026/27 Champions League fixtures and results.
