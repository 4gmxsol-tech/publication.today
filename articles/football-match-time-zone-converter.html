Football Match Time Zone Converter: Convert Kick-off Times

Convert a match time from a selected source timezone into another timezone using your browser’s timezone database.

Publication Today EditorialPublished October 27, 2026

Convert a kick-off time

Timezone conversion depends on the date and daylight-saving rules. Confirm the original timezone on the official fixture listing.

How time-zone conversion works

Choose the date, local kick-off time and source timezone, then select the timezone where you will watch. The conversion uses the browser’s Intl timezone data, including daylight-saving rules where supported.

Always verify official kick-off times

Enter the timezone shown by the official competition or club. If the match is rescheduled, update the date and time before relying on the conversion.
