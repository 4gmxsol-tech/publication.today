Atlético Madrid 1–2 Barcelona: Fermín López Turns the Game Around

Barcelona came from behind to beat Atlético Madrid 2–1 at the Metropolitano on November 8, 2026. Fermín López scored twice to seal a crucial LaLiga victory.

Football5 min read

Barcelona respond after Giuliano Simeone's early goal

Atlético Madrid struck first at the Riyadh Air Metropolitano, with Giuliano Simeone putting the hosts ahead in the 10th minute. Barcelona had to absorb the pressure and find a way back into a difficult away match.

Fermín López changes the momentum

Fermín López brought Barcelona level just before half-time and completed the turnaround in the second half, scoring again in the 66th minute. His brace turned a challenging evening into a major three-point result for the visitors.

What the result means

The 2–1 win gives Barcelona an important result in Matchday 12 and keeps them ahead in a tightly contested early-season title race. Atlético will regret losing their lead at home, but the performance also showed how quickly the balance of a top-level match can change.

The key takeaway

Barcelona showed resilience after conceding early, while Fermín López delivered the decisive contribution. The match is another reminder that control of possession matters less than making key moments count.

Match facts

Competition: LaLiga EA SPORTS, Matchday 12. Date: Sunday, November 8, 2026. Venue: Riyadh Air Metropolitano, Madrid. Full-time: Atlético Madrid 1–2 FC Barcelona. Atlético scorer: Giuliano Simeone (10'). Barcelona scorers: Fermín López (45', 66').
