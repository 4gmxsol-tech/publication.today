Fiber, Food and Digestive Health

Fiber-rich foods support digestive health, and increasing them gradually can make the change more comfortable.

Publication Today EditorialUpdated October 2026
⌁

Find fiber in everyday foods

Beans, lentils, vegetables, fruit, whole grains, nuts and seeds contain different types of fiber. A varied selection offers more than focusing on one food or a single supplement.

Increase gradually

A sudden large increase in fiber may cause gas or bloating. Add fiber-rich foods over time and drink fluids according to your needs. Individual tolerance differs, particularly for people with digestive conditions.

Pay attention to symptoms

Persistent abdominal pain, unexplained weight loss, blood in stool or lasting changes in bowel habits deserve medical assessment. General food advice should not replace evaluation of concerning symptoms.

A thoughtful next step

Use this guide as a starting point, not a rigid rulebook. Consider your own circumstances, look for reliable sources, and seek qualified professional advice when a decision affects your health or safety.

Editorial note: Publication Today provides general educational information, not individual medical advice. Health-related decisions should be discussed with an appropriately qualified professional.
