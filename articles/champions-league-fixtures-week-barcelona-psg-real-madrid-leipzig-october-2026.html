Champions League Fixtures This Week: Barcelona vs PSG and Real Madrid vs Leipzig

The key UEFA Champions League fixtures for 20–21 October 2026: Barcelona visit Paris Saint-Germain, Real Madrid host RB Leipzig, and El Clásico follows on Sunday in La Liga.

Publication Today EditorialUpdated October 19, 2026
F

The UEFA Champions League league phase reaches Matchday 3 this week, with two fixtures drawing particular attention: Barcelona travel to Paris Saint-Germain on Tuesday, while Real Madrid welcome RB Leipzig on Wednesday. Kick-off for both headline matches is scheduled for 21:00 Central European summer time (CEST, UTC+2).

Tuesday, October 20: Paris Saint-Germain vs Barcelona

Competition: UEFA Champions League, league phase — Matchday 3
Venue: Parc des Princes, Paris
Kick-off: 21:00 CEST

Barcelona’s European test is an away trip to PSG. The key questions are whether Barcelona can keep their passing structure under pressure, how effectively they protect the space behind their defensive line, and whether PSG can turn transitions into high-quality chances. Those are tactical talking points, not predictions of the result.

Barcelona arrive after a 4–1 win away to Galatasaray in Matchday 2, while PSG beat Manchester City 4–2. Those results set up an intriguing meeting, but they do not guarantee what will happen on the pitch.

Wednesday, October 21: Real Madrid vs RB Leipzig

Competition: UEFA Champions League, league phase — Matchday 3
Venue: Santiago Bernabéu, Madrid
Kick-off: 21:00 CEST

Real Madrid return to the Bernabéu for a meeting with Leipzig. Madrid’s 4–3 away win over Roma in Matchday 2 showed their ability to stay in a game with a high scoreline; against Leipzig, controlling transitions and avoiding unnecessary turnovers will be important themes to watch. Leipzig’s pressing and pace can make space management a decisive part of the contest.

And what about Barcelona vs Real Madrid?

There is another major date for fans this week: Barcelona are scheduled to host Real Madrid on Sunday, October 25. Important clarification: El Clásico is a La Liga fixture, not a Champions League match. It follows the midweek European games, so both clubs face a demanding run of fixtures.
