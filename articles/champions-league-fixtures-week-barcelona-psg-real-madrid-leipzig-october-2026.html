Champions League Results: PSG Beat Barcelona 4-2 as Real Madrid Defeat Leipzig 4-1

The key UEFA Champions League fixtures for 20–21 October 2026: Barcelona visit Paris Saint-Germain, Real Madrid host RB Leipzig, and El Clásico follows on Sunday in La Liga.

Publication Today EditorialUpdated October 22, 2026
F

The third matchday of the 2026/27 UEFA Champions League delivered two headline results: Paris Saint-Germain beat Barcelona 4-2 on Tuesday, October 20, while Real Madrid defeated RB Leipzig 4-1 on Wednesday, October 21. The games offered very different lessons for the Spanish sides: PSG punished Barcelona in a high-tempo contest, while Madrid pulled away from Leipzig after the break.

PSG 4-2 Barcelona: Paris take control late

Competition: UEFA Champions League, league phase — Matchday 3
Venue: Parc des Princes, Paris
Date: Tuesday, October 20, 2026

PSG claimed a 4-2 win over Barcelona. Désiré Doué opened the scoring, and Lucas Hernández added another before Vitinha and Fabián Ruiz struck late to settle the match. Barcelona responded through Xavi Espart and Lamine Yamal, but could not complete a comeback.

The result highlights the danger of allowing PSG to attack space quickly and sustain pressure around the box. For Barcelona, the two goals showed their ability to respond, but the defensive moments between PSG’s late strikes proved costly. The scoreline and scorers are confirmed in the official match record.

Real Madrid 4-1 RB Leipzig: Madrid finish strongly

Competition: UEFA Champions League, league phase — Matchday 3
Venue: Santiago Bernabéu, Madrid
Date: Wednesday, October 21, 2026

Real Madrid secured a 4-1 victory over Leipzig at the Bernabéu. Madrid’s attacking quality made the difference as the match opened up, extending their strong start to the league phase. Leipzig now need to respond in their upcoming European fixtures.

For Madrid, the margin of victory provides momentum heading into the next run of matches. For Leipzig, the priority will be improving defensive control and managing transitions against opponents who can punish turnovers.

Other notable Matchday 3 results

The wider round also produced several high-scoring games. Liverpool beat Villarreal 4-3, Manchester City defeated AEK Athens 5-1, Napoli edged Bodø/Glimt 4-3, and Como beat Manchester United 4-1. Bayern München and Arsenal drew 2-2, while Aston Villa won 3-1 against Viking.

What comes next?

The next Champions League matchday begins on November 3–4. Barcelona are scheduled to host Aston Villa on November 3, while Real Madrid travel to face AEK Athens on November 4. Check the official fixture list before kick-off in case of schedule changes.

El Clásico is a separate domestic fixture: Barcelona are scheduled to host Real Madrid on Sunday, October 25 in La Liga, not in the Champions League. Confirm the latest details with the clubs’ official schedules.
