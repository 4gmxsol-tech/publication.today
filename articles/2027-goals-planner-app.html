Frequently asked questions

Is the 2027 goals planner app free?

Yes. The interactive planner is free to use in your browser — no account, no sign-up, no payment. Your goals and habits are saved only on your device.

Does the planner save my goals and habits?

Yes — everything is stored in your browser's local storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded to a server. Use Export my plan (JSON) to back up your plan or move it to another device.

Can I print my 2027 plan?

Yes. Use the Print my plan button to print your goals, habits and weekly review.

What is the difference between the free planner app and the 2027 Goals Planner Bundle?

The free app is the interactive digital version of the same system — goals, habits, calendar and weekly review in your browser. The 2027 Goals Planner & Calendar Bundle is the printable 24-page PDF planner and calendar plus the 60-page print book — the same system, on paper.
