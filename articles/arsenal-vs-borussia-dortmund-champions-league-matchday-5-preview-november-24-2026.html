How the teams arrive

Arsenal's latest Champions League result was a 2–1 away win against Slavia Praha on November 4. They had drawn 2–2 away to Bayern München in Matchday 3. Those results give the home side a recent European reference point without implying that the next match will follow the same pattern.

Borussia Dortmund beat Real Betis 2–1 on November 4 after earning a 2–1 away win at Sabah on October 20. The visitors therefore arrive with two league-phase wins in their latest completed European matches.

What to watch at Emirates Stadium

The match brings together two teams with recent Champions League experience and different home-and-away contexts. Arsenal will look to use their home setting, while Dortmund will aim to manage the atmosphere and find opportunities when possession changes. The midfield battle, transitions and each side's response after losing the ball are useful themes to follow; they are editorial talking points, not confirmed tactical instructions.
