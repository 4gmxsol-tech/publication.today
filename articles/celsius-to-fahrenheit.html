Celsius to Fahrenheit: Formula, Examples and Quick Conversion Chart

Need to convert a weather forecast, oven temperature or science measurement? Here is the simple formula, a few common examples and a quick converter.

Publication Today EditorialPublished November 11, 2026

Convert Celsius to Fahrenheit


20°C = 68°F

What is the Celsius to Fahrenheit formula?

Multiply the temperature in Celsius by 9/5 (or 1.8), then add 32. The formula is: °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32.

For example, to convert 20°C: 20 × 1.8 = 36, then 36 + 32 = 68°F.

How do you convert Fahrenheit back to Celsius?

Subtract 32 from the Fahrenheit value, then multiply by 5/9. The reverse formula is °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9. For example, 68°F becomes (68 − 32) × 5/9 = 20°C.

Why do weather apps use different temperature scales?

Celsius is used by most countries and in scientific contexts, while Fahrenheit remains common in everyday weather reporting in the United States. The two scales describe the same physical temperature with different reference points and interval sizes.

Frequently asked questions

What is a quick way to estimate Celsius in Fahrenheit?

For a rough mental estimate, double the Celsius value and add 30. This is only an approximation; use the exact formula when precision matters.

Is 20°C hot or cold?

20°C equals 68°F and is often described as mild or comfortable, although how it feels depends on humidity, wind, clothing and personal preference.

What is 180°C in Fahrenheit for an oven?

180°C equals 356°F. Many ovens use rounded settings, so recipes may list this as approximately 350°F.

Sources and accuracy

The formulas follow the standard relationship between the Celsius and Fahrenheit temperature scales. For scientific or safety-critical work, use a calibrated instrument and the precision required by the task.
