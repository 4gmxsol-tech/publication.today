What the Skin Barrier Does — and How to Treat It Gently

The skin barrier helps limit water loss and protect skin. A simpler routine can help when products become irritating.

Publication Today EditorialUpdated October 2026
◌

A protective outer layer

The outermost skin layer helps retain moisture and provides a barrier against the environment. Harsh cleansing, excessive exfoliation and irritating products can disrupt comfort and leave skin feeling tight, dry or reactive.

Simplify when skin feels stressed

Consider pausing optional active products and using a gentle cleanser and moisturizer while you assess what changed. Avoid scrubbing or adding multiple corrective products in response to irritation.

Persistent symptoms deserve care

Cracking, severe pain, swelling or a rash that does not improve may need professional assessment. The same visible symptom can have different causes, so online routines cannot reliably diagnose a skin condition.

A thoughtful next step

Use this guide as a starting point, not a rigid rulebook. Consider your own circumstances, look for reliable sources, and seek qualified professional advice when a decision affects your health or safety.
