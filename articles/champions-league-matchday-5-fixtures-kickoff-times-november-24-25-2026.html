How to convert Champions League kick-off times to US Eastern, US Pacific and Sudan

UEFA publishes the league-phase schedule in Central European Time (CET) and states that matches begin at 21:00 unless an earlier time is shown. On these dates CET is UTC+1, Sudan is UTC+2, US Eastern is Eastern Standard Time (EST, UTC−5), and US Pacific is Pacific Standard Time (PST, UTC−8), because US daylight saving time ended on November 1, 2026. An 18:45 CET kick-off is 12:45 p.m. EST, 9:45 a.m. PST, 17:45 UTC and 19:45 in Sudan; a 21:00 CET kick-off is 3:00 p.m. EST, 12:00 p.m. PST, 20:00 UTC and 22:00 in Sudan.
