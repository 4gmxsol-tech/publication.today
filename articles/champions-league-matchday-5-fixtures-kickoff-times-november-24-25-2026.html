How to convert the kick-off times

UEFA publishes the league-phase schedule in Central European Time (CET) and states that matches begin at 21:00 unless an earlier time is shown. On these dates CET is UTC+1, while Sudan is UTC+2. That means an 18:45 CET kick-off is 17:45 UTC and 19:45 in Sudan; a 21:00 CET kick-off is 20:00 UTC and 22:00 in Sudan.

Which Matchday 5 games start at 18:45 CET?

Four fixtures have the earlier 18:45 CET start: Bodø/Glimt vs LASK and Galatasaray vs Aston Villa on November 24, followed by Sabah vs Barcelona and Slavia Praha vs Villarreal on November 25. The other 14 matches are listed for 21:00 CET.
