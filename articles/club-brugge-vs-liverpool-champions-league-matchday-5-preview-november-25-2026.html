How the teams arrive

Club Brugge's latest league-phase result was a 2–1 away win over PSV Eindhoven on November 4. Before that, the Belgian side beat Lens 3–0, won 4–3 away at Inter and lost 3–2 at home to Aston Villa. That run gives the hosts three wins from their first four league-phase matches.

Liverpool's latest Champions League result was a 3–2 defeat away to Fenerbahçe on November 4. The Reds had beaten Villarreal 4–3 on October 20, won 6–3 away at LASK on October 14 and opened their league phase with a 2–1 win over Atlético de Madrid on September 9.

League-phase points before Matchday 5

Both clubs had nine points after four league-phase matches in the table shown on Liverpool's official match centre on November 11. Each had three wins and one defeat. This is a dated snapshot before Matchday 5, not a prediction or a guarantee of their final positions.
