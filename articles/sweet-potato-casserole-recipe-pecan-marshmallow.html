Sweet Potato Casserole Recipe: Pecan or Marshmallow Topping

Make a classic sweet potato casserole with a smooth cinnamon filling and choose a buttery pecan streusel or toasted marshmallow topping.

Publication Today EditorialPublished November 17, 2026

At a glance

Side dish · About 1 hour 20 minutes including baking

Ingredients

  • 3 lb sweet potatoes
  • ¼ cup melted butter
  • ⅓ cup milk
  • 2 tbsp maple syrup or brown sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 1 large egg, optional
  • Pecan topping: ¾ cup chopped pecans, ⅓ cup brown sugar, ¼ cup flour, 3 tbsp butter; OR 2 cups mini marshmallows

A classic holiday side, made your way

This sweet potato casserole has a soft, lightly spiced centre and a topping that can be crunchy, sweet or both. The base recipe serves about 8 as a side dish. Choose pecans for a crisp streusel, marshmallows for a familiar sweet finish, or divide the dish and make both.

Ingredients

For the filling: 3 pounds (about 1.4 kg) sweet potatoes; ¼ cup melted butter; ⅓ cup milk; 2 tablespoons maple syrup or brown sugar; 1 teaspoon vanilla extract; ½ teaspoon ground cinnamon; ¼ teaspoon salt; and 1 large egg, lightly beaten (optional, for a firmer set). For the pecan topping: ¾ cup chopped pecans, ⅓ cup brown sugar, ¼ cup all-purpose flour and 3 tablespoons softened butter. For a marshmallow topping, use about 2 cups mini marshmallows instead.

Prepare the sweet potatoes

Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Scrub the sweet potatoes, prick them with a fork and bake on a lined tray until very tender, usually 45–60 minutes depending on size. Let them cool enough to handle, then remove the skins. Baking keeps the filling less watery than boiling, though peeled cubes can be boiled until tender if you are short on time; drain them very well.

Mix the filling

Mash the warm sweet potato flesh until mostly smooth. Stir in melted butter, milk, maple syrup or brown sugar, vanilla, cinnamon and salt. Taste and adjust the sweetness. If using egg, let the mash cool slightly before mixing it in so it does not scramble. Spread the filling in a lightly buttered 2-quart baking dish.

Add a pecan or marshmallow topping

For pecans, mix chopped pecans, brown sugar and flour, then rub in the softened butter until crumbly. Scatter evenly over the filling. Bake for 25–30 minutes until hot and the topping is golden. For marshmallows, bake the filling without a topping for about 20 minutes, scatter marshmallows over it, then return to the oven for 5–8 minutes until puffed and lightly golden. Watch closely because marshmallows brown quickly.

Make-ahead and serving tips

Prepare the filling up to one day ahead, cover and refrigerate. Keep the pecan mixture or marshmallows separate until baking. If the dish is cold from the refrigerator, allow extra oven time and check that the centre is hot before adding a delicate topping. Serve with roasted vegetables, green bean casserole or a savoury main dish.

How to store leftovers

Refrigerate leftovers promptly in a covered, shallow container. USDA guidance says perishable foods should not sit at room temperature for more than two hours (one hour when the temperature is above 90°F), and refrigerated leftovers are generally best used within 3–4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165°F (74°C), checking the centre with a food thermometer.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use canned sweet potatoes? Yes, drain them thoroughly and reduce added sweetener because canned varieties may already be sweetened. Can I make it without eggs? Yes; the filling will be softer but still spoonable. Which topping is better? Pecan streusel adds crunch and balances the sweet filling; marshmallows create a sweeter, softer top. Can I freeze it? Freeze the filling without the topping for best texture, thaw in the refrigerator, then add the topping and bake.

Trusted food-safety guidance

For holiday leftovers, the USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service recommends refrigerating perishable food within two hours, using refrigerated leftovers within 3–4 days and reheating leftovers to 165°F (74°C).
