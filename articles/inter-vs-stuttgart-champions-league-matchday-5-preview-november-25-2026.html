Inter vs Stuttgart: Kick-off Time, San Siro & Champions League Preview

  • Fixture: Inter vs VfB Stuttgart.
  • Competition: UEFA Champions League, 2026/27 league phase, Matchday 5.
  • Date: Wednesday, November 25, 2026.
  • Kick-off: 21:00 CET in Italy (20:00 GMT and UTC).

How the teams arrive

Inter's latest Champions League result was a 3–0 away win against Feyenoord on November 3, with goals from Yann Bisseck, Francesco Pio Esposito and Piotr Zieliński. Before that, Inter lost 4–3 at home to Club Brugge on October 13, then beat Shakhtar Donetsk 1–0 on October 21.

Stuttgart's latest league-phase match ended in a 2–0 away defeat to Galatasaray on November 3. The German side had drawn 1–1 at home to Atlético de Madrid on October 20 and recorded wins over Slovan Bratislava and Viking in the earlier rounds.

League-phase points before Matchday 5

After four matches, Stuttgart have seven points and Inter have six, according to the standings published with Inter's official league-phase schedule. Stuttgart's total reflects two wins, one draw and one defeat; Inter's reflects two wins and two defeats. The points provide context, not a prediction of the result at San Siro.

What to watch at San Siro

Inter return to the Champions League after a convincing away win in Rotterdam and will have the home setting in Milan. Stuttgart arrive after a difficult night in Istanbul and will want a more controlled performance. The midfield contest, the way each side responds after losing possession and the opening period are useful themes to follow; they are editorial talking points, not confirmed tactical instructions.
