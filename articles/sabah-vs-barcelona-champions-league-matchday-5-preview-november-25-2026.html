Sabah vs Barcelona: Champions League Matchday 5 Preview

Barcelona travel to Baku to face Sabah on Wednesday, November 25. The UEFA Champions League Matchday 5 fixture is scheduled for 18:45 CET at Baku Olympic Stadium.

UEFA's match centre lists Baku Olympic Stadium as the venue and confirms the Wednesday fixture. FC Barcelona's official match page lists the kick-off as 18:45 CET. The time conversion above uses CET (UTC+1) and Sudan time (UTC+2).

How the teams arrive

Sabah's latest league-phase result was a 4–1 away defeat to Viking on November 4. Earlier in the league phase, Sabah beat Slavia Praha 2–0 on October 13 before losing 2–1 at home to Borussia Dortmund on October 20. Those results provide context for the club's next European test in Baku.

Barcelona arrive after two consecutive wins: a 3–0 Champions League victory over Aston Villa on November 3 and a 2–1 away win against Atlético de Madrid in LaLiga on November 8. Results can change quickly, so this preview focuses on verified recent matches rather than projecting a score.

What to watch in Matchday 5

Sabah will look to respond in front of their home crowd after the defeat in Norway. Barcelona, meanwhile, will aim to carry their recent momentum into another league-phase fixture. The contrast between Sabah's home setting in Baku and Barcelona's recent European and domestic results gives the match its main talking points.

Watch how Sabah handle Barcelona's possession phases, whether the hosts can create chances when they win the ball, and how the visitors manage the match if Sabah make the opening stages difficult. These are themes to follow, not predictions or confirmed tactical instructions.

Team news and viewing information

Confirmed starting line-ups and final squad updates should be checked closer to kick-off. This preview does not assume injuries, suspensions or a predicted XI. Use UEFA's match centre and the clubs' official channels for confirmed team sheets and licensed broadcast information in your country.
