A Simple Skincare Routine: The Essentials First

A gentle cleanser, moisturizer and sunscreen can be a more useful starting point than a crowded shelf.

Publication Today EditorialUpdated October 2026
◌

Build the basics

A simple routine often begins with a gentle cleanser, a moisturizer suited to your skin and broad-spectrum sunscreen during daytime exposure. The right products depend on skin type, sensitivities, climate and individual concerns.

Introduce changes slowly

Adding several products at once makes it difficult to identify what helps or irritates. Introduce one new product at a time and follow its directions. Patch testing can reduce uncertainty, though it cannot guarantee that irritation will not occur.

Consistency before complexity

Expensive or elaborate routines are not automatically better. If a product causes persistent burning, swelling or a rash, stop using it and seek appropriate advice. Ongoing skin concerns may need assessment by a dermatologist.

A thoughtful next step

Use this guide as a starting point, not a rigid rulebook. Consider your own circumstances, look for reliable sources, and seek qualified professional advice when a decision affects your health or safety.

Editorial note: Publication Today provides general educational information, not individual medical advice. Health-related decisions should be discussed with an appropriately qualified professional.
