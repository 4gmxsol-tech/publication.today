GTA 6 Release Date, Platforms and Price: Everything to Know on Launch Day

Grand Theft Auto VI is out today, Thursday, November 19, 2026. It unlocks at midnight local time in every region, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, with a standard edition price of $79.99. Here is the release time, the platforms, the price, and what to check before you start playing.

Publication Today EditorialPublished November 19, 2026

When does GTA 6 come out?

GTA 6 comes out on Thursday, November 19, 2026. According to Rockstar Games, the game becomes playable at midnight local time on November 19 in all regions, so the exact hour depends on where you live. In the United States it unlocks at midnight in each time zone, which means players on the West Coast can start at 9 p.m. Pacific on Wednesday, November 18, while East Coast players start at midnight Eastern. If you are comparing times across regions, our match time zone converter can help you line up midnight in one place with the time where you are.

What platforms is GTA 6 on?

GTA 6 launches on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only. There is no PlayStation 4 or Xbox One version, and the launch is single-player. If you play on an older console, you will need the current generation to play at launch.

Is GTA 6 coming to PC?

Rockstar has not announced a release date for a PC version of GTA 6. The November 19, 2026 launch covers PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and no PC date has been given. PC players should treat any other date circulating online as unconfirmed until Rockstar says otherwise.

How much does GTA 6 cost?

The standard edition of GTA 6 costs $79.99 in the United States. The game is sold as a digital download rather than on a physical disc, so check your console storage before launch day: the download is large, and a slow connection can mean hours of waiting after midnight.

How to pre-load GTA 6

Players who pre-ordered the digital edition could start downloading the game on Tuesday, November 10, ahead of launch. If you pre-loaded, the game should already be on your console and will simply unlock at midnight local time. If you did not pre-load, buy the digital edition and start the download as early as you can, and use a wired connection if one is available.

Where is GTA 6 set, and who do you play?

GTA 6 is set in Leonida, Rockstar's fictional version of Florida, and it marks a return to Vice City, the series' take on Miami. The story follows two protagonists, Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval, a duo whose easy score goes wrong and pulls them into a wider conspiracy across the state. It is the first game in the series' 3D era with a playable female protagonist. Early reviews have been very strong: the game holds a Metacritic score of 99 from more than 140 reviews.

What to know before you play

  • Check your storage first. Clear space before you download, so a full drive does not stop the install at midnight.
  • Expect a big download. Pre-loading was the easy path; a fresh download on launch day can take hours on a slow connection.
  • Plan your session. A launch-day playthrough can run long, so take breaks and keep water nearby, and if a long session is your plan, our guide to digital boundaries that feel realistic is a useful reminder.
  • Watch out for spoilers. Endings and story reveals are already circulating, so mute the keywords if you want to discover the story yourself.
