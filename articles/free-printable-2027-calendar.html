How to use this calendar with a 2027 plan

A calendar shows the dates; a plan gives them meaning. Three steps turn this page into a working year:

  • Mark the quarters. 2027 splits into four 90-day quarters: Q1 January–March, Q2 April–June, Q3 July–September, Q4 October–December. Give each goal one clear target per quarter — the method in our 2027 goals guide.
  • Protect the hard weeks. The weeks around the holidays above are the ones where routines slip. Plan lighter targets for those weeks in advance instead of discovering the slip afterwards — most resolutions fail in February for exactly this reason, as explained in why resolutions fail by February.

What kind of year is 2027?

2027 is a common year of 365 days, not a leap year, so February has 28 days. It begins and ends on a Friday. The year opens with New Year's Day on Friday, January 1, and the big shopping and holiday dates fall late in the year — Thanksgiving on Thursday, November 25, Black Friday on Friday, November 26, and Christmas on Saturday, December 25.

If you are planning around school, work or travel, the long weekends are the dates to circle first: January 16–18, February 13–15, May 29–31, September 4–6 and October 9–11 all give a three-day weekend.

Frequently asked questions

What day of the week is January 1, 2027?

January 1, 2027 is a Friday. The year starts on a Friday and ends on a Friday, December 31, 2027.

Is 2027 a leap year?

No. 2027 is not a leap year, so February 2027 has 28 days and the year has 365 days in total.

When is Easter 2027?

Easter Sunday in 2027 falls on March 28, 2027.

When does Ramadan start in 2027?

Ramadan is expected to begin around February 18, 2027, and Eid al-Fitr around March 20, 2027. The exact dates depend on the sighting of the moon and are confirmed closer to the time.

How do I print this 2027 calendar?

Open this page in your browser and use Print (Ctrl+P or Cmd+P). The calendar is laid out to print cleanly month by month, and the navigation and countdown are hidden on the printed page.
