Barcelona vs Real Madrid: El Clásico Preview, Kick-off Time and Key Storylines

Barcelona host Real Madrid at Spotify Camp Nou on Sunday, October 25, 2026. Check the confirmed kick-off time, LaLiga standings, key storylines and official sources before El Clásico.

Publication Today EditorialUpdated October 22, 2026
F

The first El Clásico of the 2026/27 season is nearly here. FC Barcelona welcome Real Madrid to Spotify Camp Nou on Sunday, October 25, in a LaLiga fixture that brings together the league’s current first- and third-placed teams.

When is Barcelona vs Real Madrid?

Fixture: FC Barcelona vs Real Madrid
Competition: LaLiga EA SPORTS, Matchday 10
Date: Sunday, October 25, 2026
Kick-off: 21:00 CET (20:00 UTC; 22:00 Sudan time)
Venue: Spotify Camp Nou, Barcelona

The clubs and LaLiga have published a 21:00 local kick-off. Spain changes from summer time to standard time on October 25, so Barcelona will be on CET (UTC+1): kick-off is 20:00 UTC and 22:00 in Sudan. Always recheck the official fixture page in case of a late change.

What is at stake in LaLiga Matchday 10?

LaLiga’s published standings list Barcelona on 27 points and Real Madrid on 21 after nine matches, with Barcelona in first place and Madrid in third. That gives the home side a six-point cushion entering the fixture, although the table may change before kick-off and one match will not settle the title race.

Three tactical storylines to watch

Barcelona’s control and defensive balance. Barcelona will want to build attacks patiently while avoiding turnovers that leave space for Madrid to counter.

Real Madrid in transition. Madrid will look for moments to attack quickly and exploit space behind the opposition defence. The quality of decisions after winning the ball could be decisive.

Recovery after Europe. Both clubs played in the Champions League during the week. Fitness, rotation and the ability to sustain intensity may affect the second half, but final line-ups are not confirmed until matchday.

Team news: verify before kick-off

Reuters reported that Vinícius Júnior received a four-match suspension that includes this Clásico. Because disciplinary status and squad availability can be updated, check Real Madrid’s official matchday communications and the competition’s latest notices before treating any predicted line-up as final. This preview does not present projected XIs as confirmed team news.

Where to follow official updates

For the fixture, kick-off time and league context, use the official LaLiga match page. You can also check FC Barcelona’s first-team schedule and Real Madrid’s October schedule. Broadcast availability differs by country; consult a licensed local broadcaster.

Quick summary: Barcelona vs Real Madrid is scheduled for Sunday, October 25, 2026, at 21:00 CET (20:00 UTC) at Spotify Camp Nou. It is a LaLiga match, not a Champions League fixture.
