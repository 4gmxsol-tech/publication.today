Barcelona 1–2 Real Madrid: Mbappé’s Late Brace Decides El Clásico

Real Madrid beat Barcelona 2–1 at Spotify Camp Nou on October 25, 2026. Kylian Mbappé scored twice, with Raphinha replying in the second half.

Publication Today EditorialPublished October 22, 2026Updated November 15, 2026
F

Real Madrid won 2–1 away to FC Barcelona at Spotify Camp Nou on Sunday, October 25, 2026, in LaLiga EA SPORTS Matchday 10. Kylian Mbappé scored in the 41st and 87th minutes; Raphinha equalised for Barcelona in the 61st minute. LaLiga recorded an attendance of 62,055.

Barcelona 1–2 Real Madrid: the goals

  • 41st minute — Real Madrid 1–0: Mbappé opened the scoring before half-time.
  • 61st minute — 1–1: Raphinha finished after a pass from Pedri to bring Barcelona level.
  • 87th minute — Barcelona 1–2 Real Madrid: Mbappé scored his second after a fast break, with Eduardo Camavinga providing the assist.

How the Clásico turned

Madrid took a first-half lead, but Barcelona responded after the break and drew level through Raphinha. The hosts pushed for a winner; instead, Madrid found space on a late transition and Mbappé finished the decisive move. The result ended Barcelona’s run of nine straight league wins at the start of the season.

What the result means in LaLiga

Barcelona remained ahead in the table, but the defeat reduced their advantage over Real Madrid to three points. It was an important Matchday 10 result rather than a title-deciding game: plenty of league matches remained, and the standings could still change.

Match facts

Final score: FC Barcelona 1–2 Real Madrid
Competition: LaLiga EA SPORTS, Matchday 10
Date: Sunday, October 25, 2026
Kick-off: 21:00 CET (20:00 UTC; 22:00 Sudan time)

Spain was on Central European Time (UTC+1) on matchday after the seasonal clock change. The local 21:00 kick-off therefore corresponded to 20:00 UTC and 22:00 in Sudan.
