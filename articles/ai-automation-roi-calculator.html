AI Automation ROI Calculator: Estimate Time Saved and Payback

How much value might an AI workflow create after software costs and setup effort? Enter your assumptions to estimate monthly value, payback time and first-year net benefit.

Estimate your AI workflow economics

Use conservative numbers. Results are estimates, not promised savings.

Estimated payback—Includes setup effort
First-year net value—After setup and 12 months of costs

Model assumptions: 4.345 weeks per average month; saved hours are valued at the hourly value you enter. Setup effort is treated as a one-time cost at the same hourly value. This does not account for taxes, adoption delays, quality changes or opportunity cost.

How the calculator works

Estimated hours saved per month = (weekly task hours × automation share − weekly review hours) × 4.345. The calculator never reports negative time savings as a benefit. Net monthly value equals the value of those hours minus the recurring tool cost. First-year net value subtracts the one-time setup cost from 12 months of net monthly value.

What should count as time saved?

Count only time actually removed from the workflow. If AI drafts a report but a person must spend the same amount of time checking every fact, the net saving may be small. Include monitoring, prompt updates, exception handling and integration maintenance.

How to use the result

Run a limited pilot, compare quality against the current process and record real time spent before and after. Start with a reversible, low-risk task. For sensitive information or high-impact decisions, confirm policy and keep appropriate human review.
