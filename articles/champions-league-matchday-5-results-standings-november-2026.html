Champions League Matchday 5 Results: Real Madrid Stay Perfect, PSG Drop Points, Lille Stun Bayern

Matchday 5 of the 2026/27 UEFA Champions League league phase is complete. All 18 fixtures were played across November 24 and 25, 2026, and Real Madrid are the only team still perfect after five rounds — 15 points from five wins — after Paris Saint-Germain dropped their first points of the campaign in a 2–2 draw with Roma.

Publication Today EditorialUpdated November 26, 2026
F

The fifth round of the league phase delivered a clear picture at the top and a shock in France. Real Madrid swept PSV Eindhoven aside 5–1 at the Bernabéu on Tuesday, while Barcelona and Liverpool both won comfortably away from home on Wednesday. The night's biggest surprise came in Lille, where the hosts came from behind to beat Bayern Munich 2–1.

Tuesday, November 24: Real Madrid hit five; Arsenal and City held

Real Madrid's 5–1 win over PSV Eindhoven was the standout result of the opening night, keeping their 100% record intact. Elsewhere, Arsenal were held 1–1 at home by Borussia Dortmund and Manchester City drew 2–2 with Napoli at the Etihad. Galatasaray edged Aston Villa 1–0 in Istanbul, Como beat AEK Athens 1–0, and Porto won 1–0 away at Feyenoord. RB Leipzig beat Lens 3–1, Bodø/Glimt beat LASK Linz 1–0, and Real Betis won 2–0 away at Slovan Bratislava.

Wednesday, November 25: Barcelona and Liverpool cruise; Lille stun Bayern

Barcelona won 4–1 away at Sabah, with Anthony Gordon scoring twice, and Liverpool won 3–0 away at Club Brugge, where Alexander Isak scored twice either side of a Florian Wirtz strike. Holders Paris Saint-Germain saw their perfect start come to an end as Roma held them to a 2–2 draw in Paris, leaving Real Madrid as the only club with a perfect record this season. Sporting CP and Manchester United shared the same 2–2 scoreline in Lisbon, despite a brace from Bruno Fernandes against his former club. Lille came from behind to stun Bayern Munich 2–1, Inter beat VfB Stuttgart 2–0, Atlético Madrid beat Viking 2–0, Fenerbahçe won 1–0 away at Shakhtar Donetsk, and Villarreal won 1–0 away at Slavia Prague.

League-phase standings after Matchday 5

Real Madrid sit alone at the top on 15 points. Paris Saint-Germain are second on 13, and three clubs share 12 points behind them. The top eight after Matchday 8 qualify directly for the round of 16; teams finishing 9th to 24th go into the knockout play-offs, and the bottom 12 are eliminated. See our Champions League tiebreaker rules guide for how teams level on points are separated.

  • 1. Real Madrid — 15 points (5 wins, 0 draws, 0 defeats; 18 goals for, 8 against)
  • 2. Paris Saint-Germain — 13 points (4 wins, 1 draw, 0 defeats; 22–7)
  • 3. Como — 12 points (4 wins, 0 draws, 1 defeat; 20–6)
  • 4. Barcelona — 12 points (4 wins, 0 draws, 1 defeat; 18–7)
  • 5. Liverpool — 12 points (4 wins, 0 draws, 1 defeat; 17–10)
  • 18. Atlético Madrid — 7 points (2 wins, 1 draw, 2 defeats)
  • 25. Roma — 5 points (1 win, 2 draws, 2 defeats)
  • 36. Slovan Bratislava — 0 points (5 defeats; 1–19)

What the results mean

Real Madrid's perfect start gives them a two-point cushion with three matchdays left, and they are the only side yet to drop a point. PSG remain second despite the draw, and their goal difference (+15) is the best in the competition. Barcelona and Liverpool both climbed into the top five with their away wins, while Bayern's defeat in Lille cost them ground at the top. At the bottom, Slovan Bratislava remain pointless after five matches, and Roma sit just below the play-off line in 25th — one place short of the knockout phase as it stands.
