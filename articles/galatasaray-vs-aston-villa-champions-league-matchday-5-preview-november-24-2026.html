How the teams arrive

Galatasaray lost their opening two league-phase matches, 3–1 away to Sporting CP and 4–1 at home to Barcelona. They responded with a 4–2 win away to Lille on October 21 and a 2–0 home win over Stuttgart on November 3. The two victories give the Istanbul side six points from four matches before Matchday 5.

Aston Villa began with three consecutive league-phase wins: 3–2 away to Club Brugge, 6–0 at home to Fenerbahçe and 3–1 against Viking. Their first defeat came at Barcelona on November 3, when the hosts won 3–0. Villa therefore arrive with nine points from their first four matches.

League-phase points before Matchday 5

The points totals are a dated snapshot based on the four completed UEFA results for each club: Galatasaray have six and Aston Villa have nine. They describe the teams' positions in the eight-match league phase before this fixture; they are not a prediction of the result or a guarantee of final qualification.

What to watch at RAMS Park

Galatasaray come into the match after consecutive wins over Lille and Stuttgart, while Villa will be looking to respond to their first league-phase defeat. The home crowd, Villa's transition play and how both teams manage the opening period are useful themes to follow.
