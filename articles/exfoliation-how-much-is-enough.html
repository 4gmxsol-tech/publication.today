Exfoliation: How Much Is Enough?

More exfoliation does not necessarily mean smoother skin. Frequency and ingredient choice should reflect tolerance.

Publication Today EditorialUpdated October 2026
◌

Understand the main approaches

Physical exfoliants use particles or tools, while chemical exfoliants use ingredients such as alpha- or beta-hydroxy acids. Both can irritate skin when used too aggressively or when combined with other active ingredients.

Start cautiously

Follow product directions and avoid applying exfoliants to broken or already irritated skin. Introducing multiple exfoliating products at once makes it harder to spot the source of a reaction.

Let comfort guide you

Persistent stinging, redness, peeling or tenderness is not a goal to push through. Pause irritating products and seek advice if symptoms are severe or ongoing. Sensitive skin may do better with a simpler routine.

A thoughtful next step

Use this guide as a starting point, not a rigid rulebook. Consider your own circumstances, look for reliable sources, and seek qualified professional advice when a decision affects your health or safety.
