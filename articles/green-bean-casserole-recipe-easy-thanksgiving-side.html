Green Bean Casserole Recipe: An Easy, Creamy Holiday Side

Make a classic green bean casserole with green beans, creamy mushroom sauce and a crisp onion topping, plus make-ahead and leftover tips.

Publication Today EditorialPublished November 17, 2026

Recipe at a glance

Prep15 minCook30–33 minServes6

Oven: 350°F (175°C). Add the reserved crispy onions during the final 5–8 minutes.

A classic green bean casserole

This simple casserole combines tender green beans with a creamy mushroom sauce and a crunchy onion topping. It makes about 6 side-dish servings and uses familiar supermarket ingredients. The method works with canned, frozen or fresh green beans, with a small adjustment to the preparation.

Ingredients

Use 1 pound (about 450 g) green beans, trimmed; 1 can (about 10.5 oz / 298 g) condensed cream of mushroom soup; ½ cup milk; 1 teaspoon soy sauce (optional); ¼ teaspoon black pepper; and about 1⅓ cups crispy fried onions, divided. For a brighter finish, add a small squeeze of lemon just before serving.

How to make green bean casserole

Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C). If using fresh green beans, rinse them under running water, trim the ends and blanch in boiling water for 3–4 minutes, then drain. If using frozen beans, thaw and drain excess water; canned beans should also be drained. Stir the soup, milk, soy sauce and pepper together in a baking dish. Fold in the beans and about two-thirds of the crispy onions. Bake for 25 minutes until hot and bubbling. Scatter the remaining onions over the top and bake for another 5–8 minutes until golden.

Fresh, frozen or canned green beans?

Fresh beans give the casserole a firmer bite and need a brief blanch before baking. Frozen beans are convenient; thaw and drain them well to avoid a watery sauce. Canned beans are the quickest option and produce a softer texture, so drain them thoroughly and fold them in gently.

Make-ahead and serving tips

For easier holiday cooking, combine the beans and creamy sauce in the baking dish, cover and refrigerate for up to one day. Keep the crispy onion topping separate so it stays crunchy. When baking from cold, allow extra time for the centre to heat through and add the onions near the end. The casserole pairs well with roasted vegetables, potatoes and a simple salad.

How to store leftovers safely

Refrigerate perishable leftovers within two hours, or within one hour when the surrounding temperature is above 90°F (32°C). Use shallow containers so food cools more quickly. USDA guidance says refrigerated leftovers should generally be used within 3–4 days or frozen for longer storage. See the official USDA guidance in the food-safety links below.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use fresh green beans? Yes. Blanch them briefly before assembling. Can I make it without soy sauce? Yes; it is optional and can be omitted. How do I stop it becoming watery? Drain frozen or canned beans thoroughly and avoid adding extra liquid. When should I add the crispy onions? Add most inside the casserole and reserve the rest for the final minutes of baking.

Trusted food-safety references

For produce preparation, the U.S. Food and Drug Administration guidance on selecting and serving produce recommends rinsing fresh produce under running water and not using soap or detergent. For leftovers, consult the USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service guidance.
