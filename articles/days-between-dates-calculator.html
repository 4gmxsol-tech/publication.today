Days Between Dates Calculator: Count Days, Weeks and Weekdays

How do you calculate the number of days between two dates?

Subtract the earlier calendar date from the later date. This gives elapsed calendar days and does not count the start date as an additional full day.

Days between dates = Later date − Earlier date

For example, December 19 is 38 days after November 11, 2026. If both the start and end dates must be counted inclusively, add one to the elapsed-day result.

How many weeks are between two dates?

Divide the number of days by seven. The calculator shows complete weeks and remaining days. For example, 38 days equals 5 weeks and 3 days.

Does this calculator count business days?

This calculator counts Monday-to-Friday weekdays in the elapsed interval, excluding the start date and including the end date. It does not exclude public holidays or apply country-specific work calendars, so its weekday total is not a business-day calculation.

Does the order of dates matter?

No. If you enter the later date first, the calculator still reports the positive difference between the two dates.

Why can date calculations differ?

Some calculators count elapsed days, while others count both endpoints. Monday to Tuesday is one elapsed day, but two calendar dates if both Monday and Tuesday are included. Check which convention a deadline or form expects.

Frequently asked questions

How many days are between two dates?

Subtract the earlier date from the later date. The result is the elapsed calendar-day difference.

How do I count days including both dates?

Add one to the elapsed-day difference when both endpoint dates should be included.

Does this calculator include public holidays?

No. It counts Monday-to-Friday weekdays only. Public holidays are not excluded, so the result is not a country-specific business-day total.

Can I calculate dates in the past?

Yes. Enter any two valid dates, whether in the past or future.

Summary

Use elapsed calendar days for date differences, divide by seven for weeks, and treat the weekday figure as Monday-to-Friday days only. It does not account for public holidays or country-specific business calendars. Confirm whether a deadline counts both endpoint dates.
