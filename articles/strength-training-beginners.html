Strength Training: A Beginner’s Guide to Getting Started

Learn the building blocks of a balanced strength session, from movement patterns to gradual progression.

Publication Today EditorialUpdated October 2026
↗

Learn the movement patterns

Many beginner programs are built around squatting, hinging at the hips, pushing, pulling, carrying and bracing. You do not need a complicated exercise list to get started; learning a few comfortable variations gives you a useful foundation.

Prioritize control over load

Choose resistance that allows you to move with control and maintain a comfortable range. Ask a qualified trainer for technique support if you are unsure. Sharp pain, dizziness or unusual symptoms are reasons to stop and seek appropriate advice.

Progress gradually

When a session feels consistently manageable, progress by adding a small amount of resistance, a repetition or a set. Keep recovery days in your schedule and avoid comparing your starting point with someone else's training history.

A thoughtful next step

Use this guide as a starting point, not a rigid rulebook. Consider your own circumstances, look for reliable sources, and seek qualified professional advice when a decision affects your health or safety.
