Champions League kick-off times in US Eastern, GMT and Sudan

UEFA lists the league-phase schedule in Central European Time (CET) and states that kick-offs are 21:00 unless an earlier time is shown. On December 8–9, CET is UTC+1, the United Kingdom is on GMT (UTC+0), US Eastern is on Eastern Standard Time (EST, UTC−5), and Sudan is UTC+2. An 18:45 CET start is 12:45 p.m. EST, 17:45 GMT/UTC and 19:45 in Sudan. A 21:00 CET start is 3:00 p.m. EST, 20:00 GMT/UTC and 22:00 in Sudan. US daylight saving time ended on November 1, 2026; see the NIST daylight-saving schedule.

Which Matchday 6 games start at 18:45 CET?

Four fixtures have the earlier 18:45 CET kick-off: Viking vs Feyenoord and Villarreal vs Sabah on December 8, followed by Real Betis vs Como and Slovan Bratislava vs Shakhtar Donetsk on December 9. The other 14 matches are scheduled for 21:00 CET.

Fixtures to keep on your radar

The schedule includes Barcelona vs Manchester City on December 8 and Arsenal vs Real Madrid, Liverpool vs Porto and Borussia Dortmund vs Inter on December 9. These are fixture highlights, not predictions or claims about confirmed line-ups. Check the clubs and UEFA for team news closer to kick-off.

What comes after Matchday 6?

The league phase continues with Matchday 7 on January 19–20, 2027, before the final Matchday 8 round on January 27. UEFA schedules all 18 Matchday 8 fixtures for the same date. The official fixture list remains the reference for kick-off changes.

Broadcast rights and match-by-match listings depend on your country. In the United States, check the official Paramount+ Champions League page for current live coverage and availability.
