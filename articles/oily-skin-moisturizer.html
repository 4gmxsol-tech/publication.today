Does Oily Skin Still Need Moisturizer?

Oiliness and hydration are not the same thing. Product texture and tolerance matter more than a one-size-fits-all rule.

Publication Today EditorialUpdated October 2026
◌

Oil is not the same as water

Sebum is an oily substance produced by skin, while hydration concerns water content. Skin can look shiny and still feel tight or dehydrated. Cleansing too aggressively may worsen discomfort without solving oiliness.

Look for a comfortable texture

Some people with oily skin prefer lightweight lotions or gels, while others tolerate creams. Labels such as non-comedogenic can be a useful starting point but do not guarantee that a product will suit every person.

Keep the routine balanced

A gentle cleanser, suitable moisturizer and sunscreen can form a practical foundation. If acne or irritation persists, professional advice can help distinguish among possible causes and treatment options.

A thoughtful next step

Use this guide as a starting point, not a rigid rulebook. Consider your own circumstances, look for reliable sources, and seek qualified professional advice when a decision affects your health or safety.
