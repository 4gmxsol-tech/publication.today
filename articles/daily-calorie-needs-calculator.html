Daily Calorie Needs Calculator: Estimate Your Daily Energy Needs

Estimate daily calorie needs from age, height, weight and activity, with clear notes on the limits of equation-based estimates.

Publication Today EditorialPublished October 27, 2026

Estimate your daily calorie needs

Adults only. Equation-based estimate, not a dietary prescription or medical advice.

What does this estimate mean?

This tool uses a commonly used adult energy equation and an activity multiplier to estimate maintenance calories. Actual needs can differ with body composition, health, age and day-to-day movement.

How to use the result

Treat the result as a starting estimate rather than a prescription. Food labels, activity estimates and equation results all contain uncertainty. Avoid aggressive restriction and seek qualified advice for individual medical needs.
