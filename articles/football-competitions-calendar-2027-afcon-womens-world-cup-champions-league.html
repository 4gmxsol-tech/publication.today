Football Competitions in 2027: AFCON, Women’s World Cup and Champions League Dates

Three major tournaments give football fans plenty to plan for in 2027. Here are the announced dates, host countries and official sources to check as the schedules develop.

Publication Today EditorialUpdated October 29, 2026

Last checked: October 29, 2026. The dates below are based on official organizer announcements available at the time of writing. Fixture times, venues and competition details can change; confirm before booking travel or sharing a match time.

AFCON 2027: 19 June–17 July

The TotalEnergies CAF Africa Cup of Nations PAMOJA 2027 is scheduled to run from 19 June to 17 July 2027. Kenya, Tanzania and Uganda will co-host the tournament, making it the first AFCON hosted by three countries. CAF’s official announcement confirms the opening and final dates; check CAF for updated host-city, stadium and match details.

Read CAF’s official AFCON 2027 dates.

FIFA Women’s World Cup Brazil 2027: 24 June–25 July

The FIFA Women’s World Cup will take place from 24 June to 25 July 2027 in Brazil. The 32-team tournament is scheduled across eight host cities. FIFA has published the match schedule; its official tournament site is the best source for qualified teams, stadium details, tickets and any updates.

Check FIFA’s 2027 match schedule.

UEFA Champions League final: 5 June in Madrid

The 2026/27 UEFA Champions League final is scheduled for Saturday, 5 June 2027 at Estadio Metropolitano in Madrid. UEFA’s published season guide also lists the knockout-stage windows. Dates remain subject to change, so check UEFA’s competition page closer to the match.

Read UEFA’s 2026/27 Champions League guide.

Quick calendar

  • 5 June 2027: UEFA Champions League final, Madrid.
  • 19 June–17 July 2027: AFCON PAMOJA, Kenya, Tanzania and Uganda.
  • 24 June–25 July 2027: FIFA Women’s World Cup, Brazil.

The two national-team tournaments overlap for part of June and July, so fans following both should double-check fixture times and local time zones.
