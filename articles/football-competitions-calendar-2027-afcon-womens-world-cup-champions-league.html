2027 Football Calendar: AFCON, FIFA Women’s World Cup and Champions League Final Dates

Plan the 2027 football calendar with confirmed dates for AFCON in East Africa, the FIFA Women’s World Cup in Brazil and the UEFA Champions League final in Madrid.

Publication Today EditorialUpdated November 15, 2026

Last checked: November 15, 2026. The dates below were rechecked against official CAF, FIFA and UEFA information available on this date. Match times, venues and competition details can change; confirm official updates before booking travel or sharing a fixture.

AFCON 2027: 19 June–17 July

The TotalEnergies CAF Africa Cup of Nations PAMOJA 2027 is scheduled to run from 19 June to 17 July 2027. Kenya, Tanzania and Uganda will co-host the tournament, making it the first AFCON hosted by three countries. CAF’s latest dated announcement confirms the opening and final dates and the three host nations; detailed match arrangements should still be checked with CAF.

Read CAF’s official AFCON 2027 date and host-country update.

FIFA Women’s World Cup Brazil 2027: 24 June–25 July

The FIFA Women’s World Cup will take place from 24 June to 25 July 2027 in Brazil. FIFA confirms a 32-team tournament across eight host cities. Brazil will play the opening match at the Maracanã on 24 June, and the final is scheduled at the same stadium on 25 July. FIFA says the full match schedule has been released and remains subject to change.

Check FIFA’s official dates and host cities and its match-schedule update.

UEFA Champions League final: 5 June in Madrid

The 2026/27 UEFA Champions League final is scheduled for Saturday, 5 June 2027 at Estadio Metropolitano in Madrid. UEFA’s current final guide confirms the date and venue; check UEFA for any later changes and ticket information.

Read UEFA’s official 2027 Champions League final guide.

Quick calendar

  • 5 June 2027: UEFA Champions League final, Madrid.
  • 19 June–17 July 2027: AFCON PAMOJA, Kenya, Tanzania and Uganda.
  • 24 June–25 July 2027: FIFA Women’s World Cup, Brazil.

The two national-team tournaments overlap for part of June and July, so fans following both should double-check fixture times and local time zones.
