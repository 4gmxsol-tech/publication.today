How the teams arrive

Manchester City’s latest completed league-phase match was a 3–2 away win against Leipzig on November 4. City had also beaten AEK Athens 5–1 on October 20. Those results show the scale of the home side’s recent European performances without suggesting that the next fixture is predictable.

Napoli’s latest league-phase result was a 4–1 away defeat to Porto on November 4. Earlier, Napoli beat Bodø/Glimt 4–3 on October 20. The contrast gives the visitors a clear reason to respond, but the previous scores do not determine what will happen at the Etihad.

What to watch at the Etihad Stadium

The match brings together City’s home setting and Napoli’s need to recover from the previous round. City will look to use the ball and their familiarity with the Etihad, while Napoli will aim to make the game competitive after the Porto defeat. The opening phase, midfield pressure and transitions after possession changes are useful themes to follow; they are editorial talking points, not confirmed tactical instructions.
