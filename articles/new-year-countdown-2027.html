New Year 2027 Countdown: How Many Days Until January 1, 2027?

The year turns on Friday, January 1, 2027. The countdown below is live — it ticks every second toward midnight on New Year's Day, in your own local time.

As this page was published on November 24, 2026, 38 days remained until New Year's Day — about five and a half weeks. That is the whole point of a countdown: not the number itself, but what the number makes you do with the weeks that are left.

Key dates on the way to 2027

Four dates sit between now and the new year. Mark them first, then plan around them:

  • Thanksgiving — Thursday, November 26, 2026. Two days after this page was published. The last big gathering before the December stretch.
  • Christmas Day — Friday, December 25, 2026. Exactly one week before New Year's Day, and the last full week of the year.
  • New Year's Eve — Thursday, December 31, 2026. The final day of 2026, and the night the countdown reaches zero.
  • New Year's Day — Friday, January 1, 2027. The first day of 2027, and the first day of the plan — not the day the plan starts being written.

For every month of the new year, the free printable 2027 calendar has the full grids, the holidays and its own live countdown, ready to print.

What to do with the weeks that remain

Five and a half weeks is enough to arrive at January 1 with a plan instead of a wish. Work in three short moves:

  • This week: pick one to three resolutions. Not ten — one to three. The 50 resolution ideas for 2027 are all written as small actions with a first step; take the one or two that make you think "I'd like to be someone who does that" and leave the rest.
  • Next two weeks: plan the first quarter only. Most resolutions fail by February because the plan tries to cover twelve months at once. The 2027 goals plan shows how to work in 90-day quarters, and the 90-day fix explains why February is where resolutions usually die — and how a quarterly plan keeps them alive.
  • The final week: put the plan where you will see it. Write each resolution as a start-goal with a first step and an if-then line for the day you slip, mark the four quarters on the calendar, and tell one person what you picked. The 2027 Goals Planner & Calendar Bundle holds all of it in one place — the review, the SMART worksheets, the 90-day plans, the weekly pages and the habit trackers.

How the countdown works

The timer counts down to 00:00 on January 1, 2027 in your own local time, updating every second. If you open this page after midnight on New Year's Day, it simply reads zero — the year has arrived, and the countdown has done its job. The same live countdown also appears on the 2027 calendar page, next to the month grids.

Frequently asked questions

How many days are there until New Year 2027?

As this page was published on November 24, 2026, 38 days remained until Friday, January 1, 2027. The live countdown above updates every second, so it always shows the exact time left from the moment you open the page.

What day of the week is January 1, 2027?

Friday. January 1, 2027 falls on a Friday, which means New Year's Eve 2026 is a Thursday and the first weekend of 2027 is January 2–3.

When does the countdown reach zero?

At midnight at the start of January 1, 2027, in your own local time. The countdown on this page counts down to 00:00 on January 1, 2027 on your device's clock.

How many weeks are left until 2027?

From November 24, 2026, about five and a half weeks remain until New Year's Day — enough for one focused 90-day-style quarter of preparation if you start now, and more than enough to pick one to three resolutions and give each a first step.

What should I do before the new year?

Pick one to three resolutions and write each as something to start, with a first step you can take this week. Mark the four quarters of 2027 on a calendar, plan only the first quarter in detail, and put the plan somewhere you will see it every week.

The number is not the point

A countdown is useful for exactly one thing: turning "sometime before January" into a date you can see shrinking. Thirty-eight days is a real amount of time — enough to choose well, plan one quarter, and arrive at New Year's Day with the first step already taken. Use the weeks.
