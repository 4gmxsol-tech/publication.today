Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade Postponed to Saturday for the First Time in 100 Years

The 100th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade will not march on Thanksgiving Day. Organisers postponed the parade from Thursday, November 26 to Saturday, November 28, 2026, because of a powerful nor'easter — the first postponement in the parade's 100-year history.

☼

New York City's most famous Thanksgiving tradition is moving to Saturday. Macy's announced the decision on Wednesday afternoon, November 25, after consulting city emergency officials and the police, as a major storm gathered over the US East Coast. Millions of people usually watch the parade in person and on television on Thanksgiving morning; this year, the centennial edition steps off two days late.

The new date and time

Under the revised schedule, the 100th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade begins at 8:30 a.m. Eastern Time on Saturday, November 28, 2026. It follows the traditional 2.5-mile route from Manhattan's Upper West Side, along Central Park West and Sixth Avenue, to Macy's Herald Square flagship store on 34th Street. The parade airs live on NBC, streams on Peacock and is also broadcast on Telemundo.

Why the parade could not go ahead on Thursday

A nor'easter was forecast to hit the Northeast on Thanksgiving Day, bringing heavy rain, sustained winds of 30 to 40 miles per hour and gusts reaching 60 to 65 miles per hour. The National Weather Service issued a high wind warning and a coastal flood warning for New York City. The parade's giant character balloons cannot fly when sustained winds exceed 23 miles per hour or gusts top 34 miles per hour — a safety rule in place since a 1997 wind-related accident in which a balloon struck a lamppost and injured spectators. Crews had already begun inflating several balloons on the Upper West Side on Wednesday evening when the postponement was announced.

What to watch on Thanksgiving Day instead

If watching a parade on Thanksgiving morning is part of your tradition, NBC plans to air the 2025 parade on Thursday starting at 8:30 a.m. in all time zones, with an encore at 2 p.m. Eastern and Pacific. The rescheduled 2026 parade then airs live on Saturday morning.

A centennial parade, a day late

This year's edition is the parade's 100th, and it features a record-breaking 100 balloons, including returning favourites such as the Pillsbury Doughboy, Spider-Man, Pikachu, Smokey Bear and PAC-MAN. New balloons include Bluey, Hello Kitty Big Apple, Underdog and Buckaroo Bob the Minion. Scheduled performers include Megan Moroney, Teddy Swims, Natasha Bedingfield, Baha Men, Blues Traveler, DEVO and Jimmy Fallon & The Roots, and the broadcast is hosted by Savannah Guthrie, Hoda Kotb and Al Roker. The parade last missed Thanksgiving Day in 1944, the final year of a three-year suspension during World War II — it has never before been postponed.
