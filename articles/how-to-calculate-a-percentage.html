How to Calculate a Percentage: Formula, Examples and Percentage Calculator

From discounts and exam scores to tips and price changes, percentages appear in everyday decisions. Learn the three formulas people use most, then check your answer with the calculator.

Publication Today EditorialPublished November 11, 2026

Find what percentage one number is of another

25 is 12.5% of 200.

What is the basic percentage formula?

A percentage expresses a value as a fraction of 100. To find what percentage a part is of a whole, divide the part by the whole and multiply by 100.

Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100

Example: 18 correct answers out of 24 gives (18 ÷ 24) × 100 = 75%.

How do you calculate a percentage of a number?

Convert the percentage to a decimal by dividing it by 100, then multiply by the number. Value = (Percentage ÷ 100) × Number. For example, 15% of 80 is 12.

How do you calculate percentage increase?

Subtract the original value from the new value, divide the difference by the original value, then multiply by 100. ((New − Original) ÷ Original) × 100. A price rising from 50 to 60 has increased by 20%.

How do you calculate percentage decrease?

Subtract the new value from the original, divide by the original value, then multiply by 100. ((Original − New) ÷ Original) × 100. A price falling from 80 to 60 has decreased by 25%.

Common percentage mistakes to avoid

  • Dividing by the new value instead of the original value when calculating percentage change.
  • Forgetting to multiply a decimal by 100 when converting it to a percentage.
  • Confusing percentage points with relative percentage change. A rise from 20% to 25% is 5 percentage points, or a 25% relative increase.
  • Trying to calculate a percentage of zero as the whole; division by zero is undefined.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate 20% of a number?

Multiply the number by 0.20. For example, 20% of 150 is 30.

How do I find the original price before a discount?

Divide the discounted price by one minus the discount rate. If an item costs 80 after a 20% discount, the original price was 80 ÷ 0.8 = 100.

What is the easiest way to calculate 1%?

Divide the number by 100. For example, 1% of 450 is 4.5.

Summary

Use part ÷ whole × 100 to find a percentage, percentage ÷ 100 × number to find a portion, and compare the difference with the original value to calculate percentage change. Keep the original value as the baseline for an increase or decrease.
