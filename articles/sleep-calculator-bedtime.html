Sleep Calculator: Find Bedtimes Based on Your Wake-Up Time

Plan a realistic bedtime with a simple sleep-cycle estimate. Choose when you need to wake up and compare several possible times to get into bed.

Publication Today EditorialPractical sleep planning

Calculate possible bedtimes

Sleep cycles are often described as lasting around 90 minutes, but they vary between people and throughout the night. These times are planning estimates, not a guarantee of sleep quality.

The estimates include about 15 minutes to fall asleep. Adjust for your own routine and how much sleep you need.

How this sleep calculator works

The planner counts backwards from your wake-up time in approximate 90-minute intervals and adds a 15-minute buffer for falling asleep. It displays options around 6, 7.5 and 9 hours of estimated sleep. Actual sleep cycles are not fixed, so use the results as a starting point rather than a precise biological measurement.

How much sleep should you aim for?

Sleep needs depend on age, health, schedule and individual differences. Many adults do best with a regular opportunity for roughly seven to nine hours of sleep, but a calculator cannot assess your personal needs or diagnose a sleep disorder.

Habits that can support a steadier sleep schedule

  • Keep wake-up and bedtime routines reasonably consistent, including on weekends.
  • Make the bedroom comfortable, dark and quiet where practical.
  • Allow time to wind down and reduce bright screens or stimulating activities before bed if they keep you alert.
  • Notice how caffeine, alcohol, late meals and daytime naps affect your own sleep.

When to seek more help

If sleep problems persist, affect daily functioning, or involve loud snoring, breathing pauses or severe daytime sleepiness, consider speaking with a qualified healthcare professional. Do not use this calculator as a substitute for medical advice.
