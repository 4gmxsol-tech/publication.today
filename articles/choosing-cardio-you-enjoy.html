How to Choose Cardio You Will Actually Enjoy

Running is not the only option. The best activity is one that suits your body, schedule, access and preferences.

Publication Today EditorialUpdated October 2026
↗

Explore different formats

Brisk walking, cycling, swimming, dancing and many sports can raise your heart rate. Consider access, weather, cost, joint comfort and whether you prefer exercising alone or with others.

Use effort as a guide

A conversational pace is a practical way for many people to gauge moderate effort, though individual circumstances vary. Begin comfortably and increase duration gradually rather than chasing intensity on every session.

Design for repeatability

Prepare clothes in advance, select a convenient route or schedule a session with a friend. Enjoyment and logistics can be just as important as the activity itself when you want a long-term habit.

A thoughtful next step

Use this guide as a starting point, not a rigid rulebook. Consider your own circumstances, look for reliable sources, and seek qualified professional advice when a decision affects your health or safety.
