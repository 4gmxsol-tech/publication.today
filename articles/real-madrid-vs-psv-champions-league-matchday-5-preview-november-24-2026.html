Real Madrid vs PSV: Champions League Matchday 5 Preview

Real Madrid host PSV Eindhoven in UEFA Champions League Matchday 5 on Tuesday, November 24. Kick-off is scheduled for 21:00 CET at the Estadio Santiago Bernabéu.

Real Madrid vs PSV: match date, kick-off and venue

  • Fixture: Real Madrid vs PSV Eindhoven.
  • Competition: UEFA Champions League, 2026/27 league phase, Matchday 5.
  • Date: Tuesday, November 24, 2026.
  • Kick-off: 21:00 Central European Time (CET), as listed in UEFA's league-phase schedule.
  • Venue: Estadio Santiago Bernabéu, Madrid.
  • Time conversion: 20:00 UTC and 22:00 in Sudan (UTC+2).

UEFA lists the fixture on November 24 and states that kick-offs are 21:00 unless otherwise noted, with all listed times in CET. PSV's official match information also gives a 9:00 p.m. start, while Real Madrid identifies the Bernabéu as the venue.

How the teams arrive

Real Madrid come into Matchday 5 after a 3–2 away win over AEK Athens on November 4. Federico Valverde scored the late winner in the 92nd minute. Madrid had also beaten Leipzig 4–1 in Matchday 3.

PSV's most recent league-phase result was a 1–2 home defeat to Club Brugge on November 4. Before that, PSV earned a 1–0 away win at Porto on October 20. Those results give useful context, but they do not determine how the next match will unfold.

What to watch in Matchday 5

The first question is how PSV respond after the Club Brugge defeat when they visit one of Europe's most demanding home venues. Real Madrid, meanwhile, will look to turn their recent league-phase wins into another strong result in front of their home support.

Pay attention to the opening phase, transitions after possession changes and how each side manages the match if the score remains close. These are preview themes, not confirmed tactical instructions or predictions.

Team news and viewing information

Confirmed starting line-ups are not available yet and should not be treated as settled this far ahead of kick-off. Check the clubs' official channels and UEFA's match centre closer to the game for squad updates, final team sheets and licensed broadcast information in your country.

Kick-off times can be displayed differently by apps using device-local settings. The UEFA schedule uses CET; if you are planning to watch from Sudan, the listed 21:00 CET start converts to 22:00 Sudan time.
