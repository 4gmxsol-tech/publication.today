Meal Prep Without Spending All Sunday in the Kitchen

A few flexible components can make weekday meals easier without requiring identical containers for every day.

Publication Today EditorialUpdated October 2026
⌁

Prepare components, not a rigid menu

Cook a grain, prepare a protein option, wash produce or make a sauce that can be used in different combinations. This approach leaves room for changing appetite and prevents every meal from feeling identical.

Choose realistic quantities

Start with two or three meals rather than planning every bite of the week. Store foods safely, refrigerate perishables promptly and follow local food-safety guidance for storage times and reheating.

Keep convenience in the plan

Frozen vegetables, canned beans and ready-to-eat staples can reduce preparation time. Meal planning should make eating easier, not become a time-consuming project that creates more stress.

A thoughtful next step

Use this guide as a starting point, not a rigid rulebook. Consider your own circumstances, look for reliable sources, and seek qualified professional advice when a decision affects your health or safety.
