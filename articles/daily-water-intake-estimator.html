Daily Water Intake Estimator: A Practical Hydration Guide

Estimate a general daily fluid starting point and learn how heat, exercise, diet and health can change hydration needs.

Publication Today EditorialPublished October 27, 2026

Estimate a daily fluid starting point

This simple estimate is not suitable for people with clinician-prescribed fluid limits.

How much water do you need?

Fluid needs vary with climate, activity, body size, diet, pregnancy and health. This estimator offers a simple planning starting point, not a universal target or medical instruction.

Listen to context

Hot weather and exercise can increase fluid losses. Food also contributes water. People with conditions requiring fluid restriction, or those taking relevant medicines, should follow their clinician’s advice rather than a general calculator.
