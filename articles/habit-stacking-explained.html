Habit Stacking: Connecting a New Habit to an Old One

Pairing a small action with an established routine can make it easier to remember and repeat.

Publication Today EditorialUpdated October 2026
☼

Choose a reliable cue

Attach a small action to something you already do, such as stretching after making tea or reviewing a priority after opening your laptop. The existing routine becomes a reminder rather than relying on motivation alone.

Keep the new action tiny

A habit that takes a minute is easier to repeat than an ambitious overhaul. Once it feels established, you can decide whether to expand it. Missing a day is normal and does not erase previous effort.

Make the pairing natural

The cue should happen reliably and the new action should make sense in that moment. If you repeatedly forget, change the cue or reduce the action instead of blaming yourself.

A thoughtful next step

Use this guide as a starting point, not a rigid rulebook. Consider your own circumstances, look for reliable sources, and seek qualified professional advice when a decision affects your health or safety.
