Rest and the Art of Doing a Little Less

Rest is not only sleep. Pauses, lower demands and activities without a performance target can create breathing room.

Publication Today EditorialUpdated October 2026
☼

Different kinds of rest

Physical rest may mean sleep or a break from exertion. Mental and social rest can involve reducing decisions, stepping away from conversation or spending time in a quiet setting. What feels restorative differs between people.

Schedule a small pause

A short break between tasks, an unhurried meal or a walk without a productivity goal can provide a reset. Small pauses are not a replacement for enough sleep or for addressing an overloaded schedule.

Look at the bigger pattern

If exhaustion is persistent, consider workload, sleep, stress and health factors rather than treating rest as a reward you must earn. Ongoing fatigue or significant changes in mood deserve appropriate professional support.

A thoughtful next step

Use this guide as a starting point, not a rigid rulebook. Consider your own circumstances, look for reliable sources, and seek qualified professional advice when a decision affects your health or safety.

Editorial note: Publication Today provides general educational information, not individual medical advice. Health-related decisions should be discussed with an appropriately qualified professional.
